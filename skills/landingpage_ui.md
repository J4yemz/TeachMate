Act as a senior UI/UX designer and product designer.

Design a modern, friendly, and professional landing page for a teacher-focused web application called "TeachMate".

Product:
TeachMate is a simple classroom management system designed primarily for elementary school teachers. Its main purpose is to help teachers manage students, track attendance, and calculate and monitor student grades in one organized system.

Target users:
- Elementary school teachers
- Teachers who want a simple way to manage their classes
- Teachers who currently rely on spreadsheets, notebooks, or manual calculations

Design goals:
- Make the interface feel friendly and approachable for elementary teachers
- Keep the experience simple and not overwhelming
- Communicate that TeachMate saves teachers time and reduces manual work
- Make grading and attendance the main visual focus
- Use a clean SaaS-style landing page with excellent spacing and visual hierarchy
- The design should look realistic and production-ready, not like a generic template

Brand:
Name: TeachMate
Tagline: Your everyday teaching companion.
Alternative supporting message:
"Manage students, track attendance, and calculate grades — all in one place."

Visual style:
- Modern educational SaaS
- Friendly but professional
- Clean white/light background
- Soft purple as the primary brand color
- Subtle blue/green accents for status indicators
- Rounded cards and buttons
- Soft shadows
- Minimal illustrations
- Accessible typography
- Plenty of whitespace
- Avoid excessive gradients
- Avoid childish cartoon styling; the audience is teachers, not children

Design requirements:
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Strong visual hierarchy
- WCAG-conscious contrast
- Accessible button sizes
- Consistent 8px spacing system
- Consistent border radius
- Reusable UI components
- Modern dashboard mockups
- Realistic teacher data instead of placeholder lorem ipsum
- Keep the landing page visually calm and easy to scan
- Use subtle animations/micro-interactions where appropriate
- Prioritize usability over decoration

Important:
The landing page should immediately communicate three core capabilities:
1. Manage Students
2. Track Attendance
3. Calculate Grades

The overall feeling should be:
"TeachMate makes everyday teacher tasks easier."

Do not make it feel like a complex enterprise school management system. Keep it focused, approachable, and teacher-friendly.
